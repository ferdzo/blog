### RTFnet
Yesterday, after a tiring studying sesh, I got on HackerNews,and the last TheSerialPort video about IRC was trending. So I opened the thread to read it and got a small throwback. Despite me being not even a quarter century old, I was active on IRC from around 2015-2016 to around 2018, and I had a blast of time there, learnt a lot about technology, met a lot of new people. It tingled me, so after couple of hours I hosted my new IRC net, RTFnet, the name was given by my close friend Tunas, the name has an origin from my amateur radio callsign Z33RTF, which has a special origin, which I would like to explain in some future post. The network is running on a machine from Hetzner, the ircd is Ergo, which happens to be a new ircd written in Go that has a lot of inbuilt features, it is fast and easy to setup and so far it's perfect. I don't expect much from it but I find it cool, having a space to communicate everything, that is not bloated is so cool, I find the simplicity and the history cool, plus it offers much to learn.

Feel free to join, just go to [irc.ferdzo.xyz](https://irc.ferdzo.xyz) and connect with your favorite irc client.
